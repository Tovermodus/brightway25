bw2analyzer

The results/analysis layer of Brightway 2.5 — takes an already-solved bw2calc.LCA object and bw2data activities and turns them into contribution rankings, activity-to-activity comparisons, tag-based aggregation, supply-chain graph post-processing, and database health checks. It performs no LCA calculation itself; it consumes what bw2calc and bw2data already produced.

Typical usage

import bw2data as bd
import bw2calc as bc
from bw2analyzer import ContributionAnalysis

bd.projects.set_current("my project")
activity = bd.get_node(name="some activity")

lca = bc.LCA({activity: 1}, method=("some", "method"))
lca.lci()
lca.lcia()

# Top contributing processes/emissions to the score just computed
ca = ContributionAnalysis()
top_processes = ca.annotated_top_processes(lca, limit=10)
top_emissions = ca.annotated_top_emissions(lca, limit=10)

for score, amount, name in top_processes:
    print(f"{score:.4g}  {name}")

# Human-readable recursive trace of where the score comes from
from bw2analyzer import print_recursive_calculation
print_recursive_calculation(activity, ("some", "method"), max_level=3, cutoff=0.02)

Where to look

I have a solved bw2calc.LCA, how do I find its biggest contributors?

contribution.py ContributionAnalysis — call .annotated_top_processes(lca) or .annotated_top_emissions(lca) for human-readable (score, amount, name) results; .top_matrix() / .sort_array() are the lower-level numeric building blocks.

How do I compare two activities, or see why their LCA scores differ?

comparisons.py: compare_activities_by_lcia_score (band-based ranking of several activities by score) and compare_activities_by_grouped_leaves / find_differences_in_inputs (diff underlying leaf-level inputs, via find_leaves / group_leaves).

How do I aggregate impacts by a custom tag instead of by activity?

tagged.py traverse_tagged_databases (single LCIA method) and multi_traverse_tagged_databases (several methods at once); tags come from an activity/exchange "tag" key (configurable label arg), falling back to default_tag when absent.

How do I turn a GraphTraversal result into a treemap / force-directed graph, or simplify it?

sc_graph.py GTManipulator — unroll_graph, simplify / simplify_naive, d3_treemap, d3_force_directed.

How do I sanity-check a whole database for data-quality issues?

health_check.py DatabaseHealthCheck(database).check() — bundles duplicate/unlinked exchange checks, uncertainty-distribution sanity checks, multi-output/self-production checks, and PageRank-based importance ranking (delegates to page_rank.py and matrix_grapher.py).

How do I get the inventory as a labeled table, or print a human-readable supply-chain trace?

lci.py get_labeled_inventory(lca) for a labeled pandas.DataFrame; utils.py print_recursive_calculation (LCIA-weighted, needs a method) and print_recursive_supply_chain (unweighted amounts only) for console output.

Related modules

bw2calc — produces the solved LCA objects (score, inventory, technosphere matrix) that bw2analyzer's contribution/comparison/health-check functions consume.
bw2data — supplies the Activity/Database objects, exchanges, and tags that bw2analyzer reads and reports on.
bw_graph_tools — the lower-level supply-chain graph traversal engine; sc_graph.py here post-processes the nodes/edges results that traversal produces.
